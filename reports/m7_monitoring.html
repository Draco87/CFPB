Monitoring replay, 2025-26

Drift, accuracy and calibration month by month, as if live.

What monitoring checks

Each test month is treated as if the models were live. Inputs and outputs can be checked every day (no answers needed); accuracy and calibration need the real outcomes, which arrive weeks later.

PSI (population stability index) measures how different a month is from the training data: below 0.1 stable, 0.1-0.25 moderate shift, above 0.25 major shift.

Predicted/actual: 1.0 = the payout percentages are right on average; 1.5 = 50% too high. Months with fewer than 30 payouts are too noisy to judge and shown as '-'.
