CFPB complaints - trends and anomalies

Complaints with a published narrative. Unusual months are compared with the median of the 6 months before them (spike = 1.5x or more, drop = half or less).

At a glance

The archive holds 3.0 million complaints that have a written narrative, from May 2021 to Feb 2026 (58 months).

They name 5,131 companies, 9 products and 93 different issues.

How many complaints, and when

A typical month by year: 2021: 17,184, 2022: 28,106, 2023: 40,618, 2024: 67,865, 2025: 101,838, 2026: 17,176.

From 2021 to 2026, a typical month went from about 17,184 to 17,176 complaints (1.0x).

What to notice
  • Apr 2022: 30,346 complaints - a spike, 1.6x the usual level of about 18,862.
  • May 2024: 71,313 complaints - a spike, 1.5x the usual level of about 46,016.
  • Jan 2025: 172,937 complaints - a spike, 2.3x the usual level of about 75,130.
  • Jan 2026: 22,227 complaints - a drop, 0.3x the usual level of about 79,896.
  • Feb 2026: 12,124 complaints - a drop, 0.2x the usual level of about 77,146.

Which products people complain about

Credit reporting dominates: 73% (about 3 in 4) of all complaints.

The next biggest are Debt collection (9%) and Credit / prepaid card (5%). The smallest is Payday / personal loan (0.8%).

What to notice
  • Because one product is so large, overall accuracy can look good even when rare products are handled badly - that is why the models are scored with macro-F1.

Which companies

The top 3 companies - EQUIFAX, INC., TRANSUNION INTERMEDIATE HOLDINGS, INC., Experian Information Solutions Inc. - receive 69% (about 2 in 3) of all complaints.

The top 10 together receive 77%.

What to notice
  • When a few companies account for most complaints, anything unusual at one of them shows up in the totals - a useful first check when a spike appears.

What the complaints are about

The most common issue is Incorrect information on your report (35% of complaints).

What to notice
  • Rising: Incorrect information on your report - 37% -> 40% of complaints (last 12 months vs the 12 before).
  • Rising: Improper use of your report - 20% -> 22% of complaints (last 12 months vs the 12 before).
  • Falling: Problem with a company's investigation into an existing problem - 18% -> 13%.
  • Falling: Other transaction problem - 4% -> 0.6%.
  • Some rises and falls are the CFPB renaming issue categories, not real changes.

How companies respond

Companies answered 99% of complaints on time (within 15 days).

The most common outcome is Closed with explanation (62%).

Only 2% of complaints ended with money paid back to the consumer.

What to notice
  • The on-time rate stayed steady - no month fell more than 5 points below normal.

Where complaints come from

The top 3 states - TX, FL, CA - account for 36% of complaints (these are also the most populous states, so this is mostly about population).

3% of complaints are tagged Older American and 6% Servicemember.

100% arrived via Web.

How long the complaints are

A typical complaint is about 592 characters (roughly 99 words).

Mortgage complaints are the longest (typical 1,127 characters); Credit reporting the shortest (540).

13% are very short (under 200 characters) - hard for any model to classify.
